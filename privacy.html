小螺 AI 用量桌宠隐私政策 / Xiaoluo AI Usage Pet Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-09-06 适用范围 / Scope: Mac App Store 版本；按 0.31.12 的数据处理行为编写 / Mac App Store edition; reflects data handling in 0.31.12

1. 处理者与联系方式 / Controller and contact

小螺 AI 用量桌宠（“小螺”）由独立开发者 Puck Shen 提供。隐私、数据删除、安全或支持问题可通过应用内“反馈与支持”，或发送邮件至 xiaoluoaiusage-feedback@outlook.com 联系。

Xiaoluo AI Usage Pet (“Xiaoluo”) is provided by independent developer Puck Shen. For privacy, deletion, security, or support requests, use Feedback & Support in the app or email xiaoluoaiusage-feedback@outlook.com.

2. 核心原则 / Summary

小螺是一款本地优先的 macOS 用量工具，没有自己的云端账号或接收凭证、用量数据的开发者服务器，也不包含广告、跟踪器或分析 SDK。只有在你主动连接服务后，小螺才会直接向对应服务商读取展示额度、用量、费用或余额所需的数据。

Xiaoluo is a local-first macOS usage utility. It has no cloud account or developer server of its own receiving credentials or usage data, and includes no ads, trackers, or analytics SDKs. After you connect a service, Xiaoluo requests only the quota, usage, cost, or balance data needed for display directly from that provider.

小螺不读取提示词、回复、代码或本地 AI 会话文件，不调用模型、不代你购买 AI 服务，也不修改服务商账号。购买小螺永久 Pro 由 Apple 单独处理。

Xiaoluo does not read prompts, responses, code, or local AI session files, call models, purchase AI services on your behalf, or change provider accounts. Purchases of Xiaoluo Permanent Pro are handled separately by Apple.

3. 在这台 Mac 上处理的数据 / Data processed on this Mac

  • 已连接服务返回的额度、用量、费用、余额、套餐和重置时间，用于应用内展示、缓存和你主动启用的通知。
  • 语言、窗口位置、桌宠大小、展示方式、隐私遮罩、已添加服务等偏好保存在 macOS UserDefaults。
  • 本地诊断摘要滚动保留不超过 30 天和 240 条事件，只记录启动、正常或疑似异常退出、连接结果、用量状态变化，以及 macOS MetricKit 提供的崩溃、卡死、CPU 和磁盘异常数量；不保存原始载荷、调用栈、凭证、账号标识、额度数值、路径、提示词或设备标识。
  • 用量通知只在你主动开启后请求 macOS 通知权限。设为隐私状态的服务使用不含数值的通知文案。

应用还会处理以下本地状态：

  • 服务商返回的账户标识、账户名称或邮箱、组织与套餐信息，用于区分你的多个连接；这些信息不是另行注册的小螺账号。
  • 各服务的提醒开关、阈值，以及按账户、指标和重置周期区分的提醒去重状态。通知中心中的实际通知由 macOS 管理；未隐藏的通知可能包含账户名称和额度，显示方式受你的系统设置影响。
  • 最近提醒记录仅含时间、服务类别和处理结果，最多保留 14 天、40 条；不包含账户名称、凭证或额度值。按服务和小时汇总的刷新次数及结果最多保留 14 天、5,000 个汇总项，不记录请求正文、账户或实际用量。
  • 本机的一次性 Pro 试用资格记录，包括试用开始、到期及用于校验时钟的时间状态；该记录保存在仅限本设备的钥匙串中，用于判断试用是否已领取及是否仍有效。
  • Apple 提供的商品价格、已验证购买及撤销状态，用于显示本地化价格、授予或恢复永久 Pro。小螺不接收你的支付卡资料或 Apple 账户密码。
  • Quotas, usage, costs, balances, plans, and reset times returned by connected services are used for display, caching, and notifications you choose to enable.
  • Preferences such as language, window position, pet size, display mode, privacy masking, and added services are stored in macOS UserDefaults.
  • A local diagnostic summary keeps no more than 30 days and 240 events. It records only launches, clean or possible unexpected exits, connection results, usage-state changes, and counts of crash, hang, CPU, and disk diagnostics supplied by macOS MetricKit. It excludes raw payloads, call stacks, credentials, account identifiers, quota values, paths, prompts, and device identifiers.
  • macOS notification permission is requested only after you enable usage alerts. Services marked private use notification text without numeric values.

The app also processes these local states:

  • Account identifiers, names or email addresses, organization details, and plans returned by providers to distinguish your connections. These do not create a separate Xiaoluo account.
  • Per-service alert switches and thresholds, and deduplication state keyed by account, metric, and reset period. macOS manages delivered notifications. Unmasked notifications may include account names and quota information, subject to your system notification settings.
  • Recent alert records contain only time, service category, and processing outcome, with a limit of 14 days and 40 entries; they exclude account names, credentials, and quota values. Refresh counts and outcomes aggregated by service and hour are kept for up to 14 days and 5,000 buckets, without request bodies, accounts, or actual usage values.
  • A device-local, one-time Pro trial eligibility record, including trial start, expiry, and time-validation state. It is stored in a device-only Keychain item to determine whether the trial has been claimed and is still valid.
  • Product pricing and verified purchase or revocation status supplied by Apple to display localized prices and grant or restore Permanent Pro. Xiaoluo does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password.

4. 凭证与网络请求 / Credentials and network requests

  • OAuth Token 与 API、Admin、Management Key 按产品和账户分别保存在仅限本设备、且仅在 Mac 解锁时可读取的 macOS 钥匙串项目中。凭证不会显示在界面、复制到诊断记录或在不同产品间共用。
  • 连接和刷新时，凭证只会直接发送给对应服务商，用于读取账户、套餐、额度、用量、费用或余额；请求不经过小螺服务器。
  • 某些服务可能要求权限较高的 Admin 或 Management Key，或返回较宽的 OAuth 权限。小螺只使用其中与只读用量和费用查询有关的能力；具体权限及兼容性风险会在对应连接页面说明。
  • 需要浏览器授权的服务可能临时监听本机环回端口接收 OAuth 回调，完成、取消或超时后停止。小螺自己的用量请求会话不保存 Cookie 或磁盘缓存，响应大小受限，HTTPS 跳转不得离开原始来源。你用于登录的浏览器及服务商网站可能按各自设置保存 Cookie 和登录状态，不受这项应用内会话限制控制。
  • 应用更新由 Mac App Store 提供；小螺不会自行下载或执行更新包。
  • OAuth tokens and API, Admin, or Management keys are stored separately by product and account in device-only macOS Keychain items readable only while the Mac is unlocked. Credentials are not displayed, copied into diagnostics, or shared across products.
  • When connecting or refreshing, a credential is sent only to the matching provider to read account, plan, quota, usage, cost, or balance information. Requests do not pass through a Xiaoluo server.
  • Some services require an Admin or Management key with broader authority, or return broad OAuth scopes. Xiaoluo uses only the capabilities needed for read-only usage and cost queries. The matching connection page explains relevant permissions and compatibility risks.
  • Browser authorization may temporarily use a local loopback port for an OAuth callback and stops listening after completion, cancellation, or timeout. Xiaoluo's own usage-request sessions keep no cookie or disk cache, responses are size-limited, and HTTPS redirects cannot leave the original origin. The browser and provider website used for sign-in may retain cookies and login state under their own settings; they are not governed by these app-session restrictions.
  • App updates are delivered by the Mac App Store. Xiaoluo does not download or execute update packages itself.

5. 第三方服务 / Third-party services

你主动连接的每项 AI 服务仍受该服务商自己的条款和隐私政策约束。服务商的授权方式、接口、限制和数据格式可能变化或暂时不可用；部分额度接口属于兼容性接入，不能保证长期稳定。小螺是独立应用，与所列服务商不存在隶属、认可或赞助关系。当前支持范围和服务商隐私政策入口以应用内“连接管理”和“关于”为准。

Every AI service you choose to connect remains governed by that provider's own terms and privacy policy. Authorization flows, APIs, limits, and data formats may change or become unavailable; some quota integrations are compatibility-sensitive and cannot be guaranteed to remain stable. Xiaoluo is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by listed providers. The current supported scope and provider privacy-policy links appear under Connections and About in the app.

6. 保存、删除与撤回 / Retention, deletion, and withdrawal

偏好会保留到你修改或重置小螺；凭证会保留到你断开对应连接、重置小螺或手动删除钥匙串项目。断开单个账户只移除该连接在小螺中的凭证和缓存，不影响同一服务的其他账户；断开或清除也会使旧的进行中请求失效。

每次失去 Pro 使用资格后，超出免费版范围的连接会暂停，并在本机保留 3 天。期间恢复 Pro 可继续使用；到期仍未恢复时，小螺会在运行并执行清理时移除这些额外连接及其凭证。应用退出或 Mac 关机期间不会有开发者服务器代为执行删除。清理失败时应用会保留失败状态并重试，不把未删除的数据报告为已删除。

本地诊断摘要按上述期限滚动清理，也可在“关于 → 反馈与支持”中清除。重置小螺会断开全部服务，并在凭证清理成功后删除小螺偏好、诊断和提醒状态，停止防休眠任务。本机试用资格记录会保留，防止重置或重新安装后重复领取体验；不会据此建立云端身份或跨设备跟踪。仅删除应用文件不保证同时删除钥匙串或偏好。

上述操作不会删除服务商账号、订阅、账单、App Store 购买记录或原始文件，也不会重置 macOS 管理的登录项与通知权限。删除本地授权不等于撤销服务商侧的授权或 API Key；需要时请在该服务商后台撤销。小螺没有远程删除你 Mac 上数据的接口；本地删除问题可通过支持邮箱获得操作指引，服务商或 Apple 保存的数据需通过其各自渠道处理。

Preferences remain until changed or Xiaoluo is reset. Credentials remain until you disconnect the matching connection, reset Xiaoluo, or remove the Keychain item manually. Disconnecting one account removes only that connection's Xiaoluo credential and cache without affecting sibling accounts, and invalidates older in-flight requests.

Each time Pro access ends, connections beyond the Free tier are paused and retained on this Mac for 3 days. Restoring Pro during that period lets you continue using them. If Pro has not returned when the period ends, Xiaoluo removes these extra connections and their credentials when the app runs and performs cleanup. No developer server deletes data while the app is closed or the Mac is off. Failed cleanup remains pending and is retried rather than reported as successful deletion.

Local diagnostics roll off according to the limits above and can also be cleared under About → Feedback & Support. Resetting Xiaoluo disconnects all services and, after successful credential removal, clears its preferences, diagnostics, and alert state and stops any keep-awake session. The device's trial eligibility record is retained to prevent another trial after a reset or reinstall. It does not create a cloud identity or cross-device tracking. Deleting the app file alone does not guarantee deletion of Keychain items or preferences.

These actions do not delete provider accounts, subscriptions, billing records, App Store purchases, or original files, or reset login-item and notification permissions managed by macOS. Removing a local connection does not revoke provider-side authorization or an API key; revoke it in the provider's account settings if needed. Xiaoluo has no remote interface for deleting data from your Mac. Contact support for local deletion instructions; manage data held by a provider or Apple through their respective channels.

7. 分享、支持邮件、跨境与儿童 / Sharing, support email, transfers, and children

小螺不向广告商或数据经纪商出售、出租或共享数据，也不会自动上传本地诊断记录。只有当你主动使用反馈入口时，小螺才会准备反馈内容：先将收件人、主题与正文复制到系统剪贴板，再尝试打开可编辑的邮件草稿。这样即使未配置邮件应用，你仍可自行粘贴发送；小螺不会自动发送。你可以在发送前检查、修改或删除内容，也可复制其他文字覆盖这份剪贴板内容。剪贴板及其可能的跨设备同步受 macOS 和你使用的剪贴板工具管理。

反馈摘要可包含应用版本、系统版本及上述脱敏诊断统计，不包含凭证、账号标识、额度数值、余额、提示词、文件路径、设备标识或调用栈。你自行添加的正文或附件，以及发送邮箱地址，会由你的邮件服务商和支持邮箱服务商处理，并由开发者用于回复和排查该问题。请勿发送密码、授权码或 API Key。你可以通过同一支持地址请求删除已发送的支持内容；邮箱服务商的备份、安全及法定保留规则仍可能适用。

连接境外服务商时，请求会按照你的选择直接发送给该服务商，其处理地点和跨境安排由该服务商政策决定。小螺是开发者用量工具，不面向儿童，也不主动收集年龄或儿童信息。

Xiaoluo does not sell, rent, or share data with advertisers or data brokers, and does not automatically upload local diagnostics. When you choose a feedback action, it first copies the recipient, subject, and body to the system clipboard, then tries to open an editable email draft. This lets you paste the draft yourself if no email app is configured; Xiaoluo never sends it automatically. Review, change, or delete the content before sending, or copy other text to replace the clipboard content. macOS and any clipboard utilities you use control clipboard handling and possible cross-device synchronization.

The feedback summary may include app and operating-system versions and the sanitized diagnostic statistics described above. It excludes credentials, account identifiers, quota values, balances, prompts, file paths, device identifiers, and call stacks. Text or attachments you add and your sending address are processed by your email provider and the support mailbox provider and used by the developer to respond to and investigate the issue. Do not send passwords, authorization codes, or API keys. You may request deletion of sent support content through the same support address; email-provider backup, security, and legally required retention rules may still apply.

When you connect a provider operating outside your region, requests go directly to that provider and its policy controls processing locations and transfers. Xiaoluo is a developer usage utility not directed to children and does not actively collect age or children's information.

小螺的公开支持与条款网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。页面不使用广告、分析脚本、表单或第三方字体；访问网站时，GitHub 可能为提供和保护托管服务而记录 IP 地址等访问日志。这与应用内用量数据的本地处理不同，具体见 GitHub 隐私声明。

The public support and legal website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its pages use no advertising, analytics scripts, forms, or third-party fonts. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses and related access information to provide and protect its hosting service. This is separate from the app's local handling of usage data; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. 安全与政策变更 / Security and policy changes

隐私遮罩用于减少界面、通知及无障碍输出中的用量披露，不是停止联网或防止截图的安全边界。需要停止读取时，请断开对应连接。

Privacy masking reduces usage disclosure in the interface, notifications, and accessibility output. It does not stop network access or guarantee screenshot prevention. Disconnect a service to stop reading its usage.

小螺使用 Mac App Store 沙盒与签名、设备专属钥匙串、最小化本地读取和受限 HTTPS 请求等措施，但任何软件都无法保证绝对安全。若数据处理方式发生重大变化，小螺会在发布前同步更新应用内说明、本政策和生效日期。

Xiaoluo uses Mac App Store sandboxing and signing, device-only Keychain items, minimized local reads, and bounded HTTPS requests, but no software can guarantee absolute security. Material changes to data handling will update the in-app disclosure, this policy, and its effective date before release.
