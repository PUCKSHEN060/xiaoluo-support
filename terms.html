额度通知是可选的 Pro 辅助功能，需要相应使用资格、应用与服务开关、macOS 通知权限及可判断的最新数据。百分比按剩余额度降到或低于设置值判断，多个账户和额度周期分别判断，并抑制同一指标在恢复或新周期前的重复提醒。金额提醒按服务分别使用余额、后付费剩余或本月 API 费用；费用达到或超过所设金额时提醒，小螺不进行汇率换算。

Quota alerts are an optional Pro feature requiring eligible access, enabled app and service switches, macOS permission, and fresh eligible data. Percentage alerts use remaining quota at or below the configured threshold, evaluating accounts and quota periods separately and suppressing repeats for the same metric until recovery or a new period. Depending on the service, monetary alerts use balance, postpaid remaining, or monthly API cost; cost alerts apply at or above the selected amount. Xiaoluo does not convert currencies.

5. 免费版、体验与购买 / Free, trial, and purchase

免费版没有使用期限，当前可使用一个服务的一个账户及基础额度查看和刷新功能。可选的 14 天 Pro 体验必须由你主动开始，每台 Mac 可领取一次，不会自动续订、转为付费或产生后续扣款。本机试用资格记录在重置小螺后仍会保留；重新安装不承诺提供新的体验资格。

The Free tier has no time limit and currently supports one account for one service with basic quota viewing and refresh. The optional 14-day Pro trial starts only when you choose it and is available once per Mac. It does not renew, convert to a paid product, or create a later charge. The local trial eligibility record remains after resetting Xiaoluo; reinstalling does not promise a new trial.

每次试用到期、购买被撤销或其他原因导致失去 Pro 后，Pro 专属功能暂停，超出免费版范围的连接在本机保留 3 天。期间恢复 Pro 可继续使用；到期未恢复时，应用会在运行并执行清理时移除这些额外连接及其凭证，之后需重新连接。每次重新失去 Pro 都会重新计算该次 3 天保留期。这不会删除服务商账号、订阅或账单。

Each time Pro access ends, including trial expiry or purchase revocation, Pro-only features pause and connections beyond the Free tier are retained locally for 3 days. Restoring Pro during that period lets you continue using them. Otherwise, the app removes those extra connections and their credentials when it runs and performs cleanup; they must then be reconnected. A new loss of Pro starts a new 3-day retention period.
